# Product Brief

## Target User

Medium-sized e-commerce companies or online marketplaces that handle frequent customer support queries and already maintain support policies, FAQs, return/refund procedures, and other customer-service documentation.

The system will primarily serve two user groups:
- Customers who need fast and accurate support.
- Support/admin teams that manage company knowledge and handle unresolved issues.

## Main Problem

Customers often struggle to find the correct process for issues such as refunds, returns, cancellations, account problems, or payment concerns.

Support teams may take hours or days to respond, while FAQs and policy pages can be difficult to search or may not clearly answer a customer's exact question.

The platform will help customers find relevant guidance quickly by using the company's provided policies and support documents as the source of knowledge.

## Top 3 Workflows

1. A customer asks a support question in natural language, and the system retrieves relevant company knowledge and returns a grounded answer.

2. The system provides clear step-by-step guidance for common support procedures such as refunds, returns, cancellations, or account-related problems.

3. An admin or support user uploads or updates company policies, FAQs, and support documents so the system can use the latest available knowledge when answering future questions.

## Success Criteria

The first version will be considered useful if:

- Customers receive answers significantly faster than waiting for manual support.
- Answers are based on the company's provided knowledge and include relevant source references where possible.
- Customers can successfully follow the guidance for common support issues.
- Customer feedback and escalation rates can be recorded and measured.
- System response time can be monitored.

## Non-goals

The first version will intentionally remain limited in scope.

It will not include:

- Image, audio, or video-based support.
- Multilingual support.
- Advanced autonomous or agentic actions.
- Complex enterprise permission systems.
- Support for knowledge that has not been explicitly provided and processed by the system.
